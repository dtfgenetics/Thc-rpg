# THC: Pheno Quest — Cannabis Parody World Bible

## Purpose

Keep the game cannabis-themed, original, parody-safe, and mechanically useful. Every region, item, NPC, move, obstacle, and interaction should feel like it belongs to THC: Pheno Quest rather than a generic RPG.

## Locked Main Character

The main playable overworld character is **Seed Man**.

Seed Man is the player-controlled hero who explores the world, talks to NPCs, picks up items, uses key tools, and starts battles. Strain companions are his recruitable battle party.

## Core Tone

The game should feel like:

```text
Cannabis fantasy adventure
Grower culture parody
Light comedy
Original RPG world
Adult 21+ THC community flavor
```

It should not copy protected characters, exact layouts, logos, names, or storylines from Zelda, Final Fantasy, Legend of Dragoon, Pokémon, or any other game.

## World Identity

The world is a cannabis-fantasy ecosystem where genetics, grow tools, terpene forces, resin ruins, kief caves, and pheno spirits shape the land.

## Main Character

### Seed Man

Role:
- main playable overworld avatar
- party leader
- young seed hero / pheno hunter
- explores regions and restores lost genetics

Seed Man actions:
- walk
- interact
- pick up items
- use key tools
- trigger battles
- unlock paths
- talk to NPC growers
- lead strain companions

## Companion Identity

Strain companions are recruitable party members.

Examples:
- Blue Mango
- Sour Diesel
- Granddaddy Purple
- Skunk Scout
- Kush Bruiser

They battle for Seed Man in turn-based combat.

## Starter Region

### Grower’s Grove

Purpose:
The tutorial area where Seed Man learns movement, item pickup, key-tool use, and the first battle.

Required interactions:
- pick up Terp Tonic
- pick up Grinder Relic
- clear Brittle Resin Wall
- reveal Hidden Smoke Path with Vapor Lens
- challenge Rival Grower Ashtray

## Parody-Safe Design Rules

Use inspiration at the system level only.

Allowed:
- item-gated exploration
- turn-based party combat
- timed attack inputs
- transformation meters
- fantasy region progression

Not allowed:
- copied character names
- copied franchise logos
- copied exact maps
- copied storylines
- copied move names
- copied creature designs

## Naming Style

Names should use cannabis/grower wordplay without becoming too silly.

Good examples:
- Rival Grower Ashtray
- Terp Tonic
- Grinder Relic
- Vapor Lens
- Brittle Resin Wall
- Grower’s Grove

Avoid:
- direct copies from existing game franchises
- strain names that are fake unless deliberately marked as fictional/parody
- illegal sales language
- platform-risky wording around buying/selling cannabis

## Asset Rule

Every asset must connect to a mechanic.

Examples:
- Seed Man sprite connects to movement and interaction.
- Grinder Relic icon connects to clearing resin walls.
- Resin Wall obstacle connects to tool-gated progression.
- Blue Mango portrait connects to party and battle UI.

No random decorative art should be prioritized before it has a gameplay purpose.
